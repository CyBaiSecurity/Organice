# Daily guide

The guide is a plan for the day. It is not a task list, and nothing on it becomes overdue.

Times are Philippine time. The guide does not add events to the phone calendar.

## Open Routine

Routine is its own tab. Open it to see the day. The dashboard no longer holds the whole guide.

An alert saved by an older version still opens the previous address. That address brings you to Routine.

## First time

1. Open Routine and choose **Preview the guide**.
2. Pick a weekday. Each row shows the time and the title. Choose **Instructions** when you want the full text for that part of the day.
3. Workout is optional and sits in its own section. Leave **No workout habit**, create a Monday/Wednesday/Friday workout, or link a habit you already have. Opening the choices does not change the one that is selected.
4. Choose **Save on this phone**. Nothing is written before that.

Saving copies the week onto this phone. You can edit or delete any part of it. Alerts stay off until you turn one on. Saving again does not put the original week back, and it does not turn a guide you switched off back on. It also keeps the workout choice already stored, and it does not edit a habit you already had.

## Today

Routine shows the day as a timeline. A row keeps the time and the title. Tap the row to read the full instructions. What is happening now, and what is next, stays visible above the list. An optional work suggestion does not fill the day. A meeting without a confirmed time is not shown as an appointment. Its instructions are the times you can still propose.

Choose task, free period, and focus sit with the selected task. Adjust today holds no classes, home now, and home late. Alerts have their own details.

**Choose task** uses a task you already have. The list shows titles, fifty at a time, and the next page replaces that page. **Use** keeps an unfinished task from an earlier day only when you tap it. A finished or missing task asks you to choose another one. **Next step** on the task is separate from notes.

**Focus on this task** and **Free period** open Focus with that task selected. Focus does not start until you start it. If the day has no task, free period asks you to choose one.

These change today only:

- **No classes today** hides the school and travel that have not finished, and leaves one short optional work suggestion.
- **Home now** records the time you arrived. The first hour at home starts from that time.
- **Home late** only marks the day as late. The evening stays food, chores, and a short check-in. It does not invent an arrival time. An arrival you already saved with **Home now** stays.

**Undo home late** removes only the late mark. **Undo home now** clears only the arrival time. If more than one day change is on, the longer undo clears those changes and leaves the chosen task. Saturday and Sunday do not offer class or commute actions.

Previous and next only change the date you are reading.

## Edit the week

The weekday control only filters the list. A part checked for Monday and Wednesday is one shared part. Editing it changes both days. It does not make a separate copy for each day.

The list shows the time, the title, and whether that part is hidden or still needs a confirmed time. Each row has one **Edit**. Edit replaces the list with that part’s form. **Back to the list** or **Close** returns to the day. Reorder is a separate step. **Up** and **Down** change the order once you are reordering.

You can add a part of the day, rename it, rewrite the instructions, pick its weekdays, give it a fixed time or leave it untimed, hide it, or delete it after you confirm. **Confirm this time** saves the title, instructions, weekdays, visibility, and the time you set, and it leaves the alert off. A meeting stays unconfirmed until you do that. Renaming it does not confirm it. Parts of the day with no fixed time cannot send an alert.

**Turn the guide off** keeps your edits. Saving the guide again does not rebuild the week.

## Alerts

Alerts are off until you turn one on for a part of the day that has a fixed time. Organice keeps the next seven days of alerts. If the app stays closed longer than that, alerts stop until you open it again. **Refresh alerts** fills that week again.

## Sunday review

**Review the week** on Routine is available any day.

The review has three notes: what got finished, what needs attention, and what felt too crowded. You write those yourself. Nothing on the review is counted for you. It also lets you choose an open task for the Monday after that week, and it shows that Monday’s date. The task list stays closed until you open it. Saving the notes alone leaves Monday’s task as it is, including a task that is now finished or deleted. Choose a task, or choose **Don’t assign a task**, when you want to change Monday.

Reviews are Monday through Sunday. They are separate from habit statistics.

## Not checked on a phone

The times stored with the source week, how the task list pages in memory, and this layout were not tried on a phone. This is a source prerelease.
